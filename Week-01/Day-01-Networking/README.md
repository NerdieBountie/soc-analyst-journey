# Day 1 – Networking Fundamentals

## Objective

Understand the role of a SOC Analyst and the basics of computer networking.

## Topics Covered

- What a SOC Analyst does
- TCP vs UDP
- DNS
- Common Ports
- Introduction to Wireshark

## Key Learnings

- TCP is reliable and connection-oriented.
- UDP is faster but does not guarantee delivery.
- DNS translates domain names into IP addresses.
- Ports identify network services.

## Reflection

Today I began my journey into cybersecurity by learning the networking concepts that form the foundation of SOC analysis.
