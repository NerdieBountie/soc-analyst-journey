# Why I'm Transitioning into Cybersecurity

## Background

My professional background includes Product Management, Quality Assurance (QA), Data Analysis, and Operations. Through these roles, I developed strong analytical, problem-solving, and investigative skills.

## Why Cybersecurity?

I enjoy solving complex problems, investigating root causes, and working with data to make informed decisions. Cybersecurity—particularly Security Operations (SOC)—aligns with these strengths because it combines technical analysis with structured incident investigation.

## Transferable Skills

- Product Management → Risk assessment, stakeholder communication, prioritization
- Quality Assurance → Attention to detail, testing methodologies, root cause analysis
- Data Analysis → Data interpretation, pattern recognition, reporting
- Operations → Process improvement, documentation, incident coordination

## My Goal

I'm completing a structured 60-day hands-on roadmap to build practical SOC Analyst skills through networking, Windows security, Linux, Wireshark, SIEM, incident response, and threat hunting.

This repository documents my progress, labs, investigations, and reflections as I prepare for an entry-level SOC Analyst role.
